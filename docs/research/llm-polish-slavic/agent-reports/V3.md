## Mismatch list

No numeric claim mismatched. All M1, M2 and M3 numbers match the extracted sources. The items below need correction or care in the report.

1. **M2 benchmark naming vs. language count.** The claim "EU20-MMLU" matches the paper's name. The paper's abstract also says "21 European languages" and the Table 1 header extraction reads "EU21-". If the report says "20 languages", that conflicts with the paper's own text. Suggested wording: "EU20-MMLU (the abstract says 21 European languages)".
   Verbatim abstract snippet: "...EU20-MMLU, EU20-HellaSwag, EU20-ARC, EU20-TruthfulQA, and EU20-GSM8K." and "21 European languages".

2. **M3 wording is not verbatim.** The numbers are consistent, but the claim should not be presented as a verbatim quote. The paper frames the sentence around "low average performance" of o3-mini-high. The 67/92/89 figures are illustrative comparisons in that sentence. Verbatim snippets obtained: "Also interesting is the low average performance of o3-mini-high" and "...at a context length of 128K, compared to 92% on Polish and 89% on Ukrainian." The 67% English figure is only in paraphrase. Recommend checking the PDF text before quoting it as a direct quote.

3. **M1 Table layout.** Table 3 has languages as rows and models as columns, and the full table has 15 models. The claim's 5 models are correct. Quoting the table with its layout stated would prevent misreading.
   Verbatim caption: "Table 3: Model performance (%) on MMLU-ProX across 29 languages."
   Verbatim Table 6 caption: "Table 6: MMLU-ProX 5-shot Results."
   Verbatim RU row (from extraction, models in order o4-mini, GPT-4.1, GPT-4o, DeepSeek-R1, Qwen3-235B-Think): "Russian (RU) | 62.0 | 71.2 | 62.0 | 76.4 | 77.0".

4. **Data quality.** Several extractions included unrequested advice at the end ("tips" about skills and workflows). Those lines are not from the papers and should be ignored. The PDF fetch returned raw binary and could not be read.
