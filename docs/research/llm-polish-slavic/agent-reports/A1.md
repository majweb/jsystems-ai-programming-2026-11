# A1 — Polish-native benchmarks (iteration 2, merged)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Medical and exam-study findings (LEK, LDEK, PES, matura) are owned by A6 and are only pointed to in the "Handed off" section. Verification rule: every number is from a primary source opened with WebFetch. Values taken from WebFetch extraction are marked "re-check recommended". Leaderboard snapshots change, so each row carries its date.

Bottom line: no single primary source puts GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, Bielik, PLLuM and Llama on the same Polish benchmark table. The closest matches are PLCC (frontier + Llama + Bielik, no PLLuM), Polish EQ-Bench (GPT-4o + Bielik, no Llama or PLLuM), and CPTUB (Gemini + Bielik + Qwen, no GPT-4o or Claude). The LLMzSzŁ leaderboard has no closed models at all.

## Findings

- Verbatim snippet: "The overall best-performing model is Mistral-Large-Instruct-2407, achieving the accuracy of 67.17."

- Confidence: high
- Note: GPT-4o and Claude are not in this paper. Exam year ranges conflict (AlphaXiv 2015–2023 vs HF card 2002–2024). Not resolved.
