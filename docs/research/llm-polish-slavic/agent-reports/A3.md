# A3 — Multilingual benchmarks (PL & Slavic vs EN), iteration 2

Research date: 2026-11-24. Sources: arxiv.org/html, GitHub API and raw repo pages, Hugging Face dataset cards. All values come from WebFetch summaries of the primary pages, not a raw-text check. Gap = PL (or other language) minus EN, in percentage points (pp). Relative gap = (X−EN)/EN.

Key negative results:
- Polish is absent from MMLU-ProX (29 languages), MMMLU (14 locales), and Global-MMLU-Lite (23 subsets).
- The OneRuler GitHub repo has no per-language results files, and Polish is not visible in the README language list.

## Findings

### F1: OneRuler (2025): Polish is the top language and English is 6th of 26 on long-context NIAH, with a small gap of about 4 pp
- Benchmark: OneRuler (adapted RULER, 26 languages, synthetic long-context tasks)
- Models: Gemini 1.5 Flash, o3-mini-high (o3-mini-2025-01-31), Llama 3.3 70B, Llama 3.1 8B, Qwen 2.5 72B, Qwen 2.5 7B (open and closed); DeepSeek-R1 (English only)
- Scores (average across all models, NIAH, 64K and 128K):

| model set | EN | PL | other Slavic | metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| average over all models, 64K+128K | 83.9% (6th of 26) | 88% (1st of 26) | not given as an average | NIAH accuracy |
| o3-mini-high, 128K only (single model, aggregate NIAH per text) | 67% | 92% | Ukrainian 89% | NIAH accuracy |

- Gap PL vs EN, average: +4.1 pp (88 vs 83.9), +4.9% relative. The paper prints "88%", not "88.0".
- Gap PL vs EN, o3-mini-high 128K: +25 pp (92 vs 67). This is a single-model figure, not an average, and it is the most dramatic number in the paper.
- Correction from iteration 1: the Ukrainian 89% is o3-mini-high at 128K, not an average over models.
- Per-language accuracy for EN, PL, RU, UK, CS, SR: NOT AVAILABLE in the paper text read. Only the averages, the rank, and two single-model numbers are printed. Figures 3 and 4 hold per-language heatmaps, but their values are not in the text.
- Repo check (iteration 2): the GitHub root listing (via API) contains only LICENSE, OneRuler/, README.md, config/, misc/, requirement.txt, run_data_generation.sh, run_data_generation_xling.sh. No CSV, JSON, JSONL or results files are visible at the root. The subfolders could not be listed by the fetch tool. The README says data goes to /BASE_DIR/MODEL/LANGUAGE/CONTEXT/TASK/validation.jsonl after generation. Language codes (pol, rus, ukr, ces, srp) are not listed in the README. It says only "26 languages" and mentions "ko" and "en" as examples.
- Author caveats: native-speaker translations (one annotator covered PL and JA); "no needle" answers are excluded from accuracy; the Polish filler is a 1934 novel; tokenizers differ across models; models often wrongly say no needle exists. The paper says "English and Chinese are surprisingly not among the top five languages."
- Source (primary): https://arxiv.org/html/2503.01996v2 (also https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01996). Code: https://github.com/mungg/OneRuler
- Location in source: Abstract; Introduction; Section 3.1 ("English and Chinese are not the highest-performing languages"); Figure 4 caption; Figure 3 (heatmap); Table 2 (Wikipedia and speakers).
- Verbatim snippet: "we unexpectedly observe that Polish is the top performer on NIAH tasks with an average accuracy of 88% across all models"
- Second verbatim (Section 3.1): "it achieves only 67% accuracy on English at a context length of 128K, compared to 92% on Polish and 89% on Ukrainian."
- Source date / version: arXiv 2503.01996 v1 March 2025; v2 dated 08 Aug 2025.
- Verification status: VERIFIED for the 88% average, the 83.9% English average, the 6th-of-26 rank, and the o3-mini-high 67/92/89 figures (quotes from WebFetch summaries; raw text not checked). Per-language rows UNVERIFIED (not published in text).
- Confidence: high for the averages and rank; medium for the o3-mini single-model figures.
