# A6 — Polish-language sources & prompting language (iteration 2, merged)

Scope note: Polish-published primary studies are mostly abstract-only or press-only through the tools available. Polish-authored studies in English journals (Scientific Reports, Cureus, arXiv) are the main verified source. Press articles are used only as leads. Cureus pages on www.cureus.com returned HTTP 403, so Cureus content was verified through PMC mirrors where available.

## Findings

### F1: On the Polish Medical Final Examination (LEK-era 2022–2023 editions), GPT-4 scored 79.7% in both Polish and English; GPT-3.5 scored higher in English than in Polish.
- Study / domain: Rosoł et al., "Evaluation of the performance of GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 on the Polish Medical Final Examination", Scientific Reports 13:20512 (2023) / medicine
- Models & scores (analysed questions per exam: 195 / 196 / 194; Tables 1–2 values at temp 0 and temp 1):

| Language | Model | S22 | A22 | S23 | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Polish | GPT-3.5 | 54.9 / 50.3 | 50.5 / 57.7 | 53.6 / 61.9 | 54.8 (my calc.) |
| Polish | GPT-4 | 76.3 / 76.3 | 82.1 / 79.1 | 81.4 / 83.0 | 79.7 (abstract) |
| English | GPT-3.5 | 59.0 / 57.4 | 60.7 / 59.7 | 67.5 / 57.7 | 60.3 (my calc.) |
| English | GPT-4 | 75.4 / 77.9 | 80.6 / 81.1 | 83.5 / 79.9 | 79.7 (abstract) |

- Sample / threshold / human baseline: 200 questions per exam (5 options); 195/196/194 analysed after excluding invalid and image items. Pass threshold: at least 56%. Medical-student averages: 84.8% (S22), 84.5% (A22), 83.0% (S23); GPT-4 below these in most settings.
- PL vs EN: Yes. GPT-4 identical mean; GPT-3.5 lower in Polish (my calc.).
- Source (primary): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10665355 (DOI 10.1038/s41598-023-46995-z; nature.com redirected to an IdP login, not fetched)
- Location in source: Abstract; Tables 1–2; Materials and methods; Discussion.
- Verbatim snippet: "GPT-4 achieved mean accuracies of 79.7% for both Polish and English versions, passing all MFE versions."
- Source date / version: received 14 Sep 2023, published 22 Nov 2023. Model snapshot not stated in fetched text.
- Verification status: VERIFIED
- Confidence: high
- Note: The medRxiv preprint (2023.06.04.23290939) reported different numbers (80.7% PL / 79.6% EN, per search summary, not fetched). Cite the published version.

### F2: On the spring 2021 LEK (196 questions), Cureus 2024 reports ChatGPT-3.5 at 50.51% and ChatGPT-4.0 at 77.55%, with a 56% pass threshold.
- Study / domain: Jaworski A., Jasiński D., Jaworski W., et al., "Comparison of the Performance of Artificial Intelligence Versus Medical Professionals in the Polish Final Medical Examination", Cureus 16(8):e66011 (2 Aug 2024), DOI 10.7759/cureus.66011, PMID 39221376, PMC11366403 / medicine
- Models & scores (Results, Table 1):

| Model | Correct | % |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT 3.5 | 99 / 196 | 50.51 |
| ChatGPT 4.0 | 152 / 196 | 77.55 |

- Sample / threshold / human baseline: 196 questions (spring 2021 LEK). Pass threshold 56% (Discussion). Human comparison: 4,779 candidates, scores 54–196 of 200, 79 failed (Discussion). The paper claims 4.0 scored "significantly below" human averages but reports no statistical test; treat that claim as unsupported.
- PL vs EN: None (interaction in Polish).
- Source (primary): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11366403/ (cureus.com article page blocked; the PDF URL https://assets.cureus.com/uploads/original_article/pdf/278818/20240802-2372588-qewv51.pdf returned binary only)
- Location in source: Results (first paragraph, Table 1); Discussion paragraphs 3–5; Conclusions.
- Verbatim snippet: "ChatGPT 4.0 answered 152 questions correctly (77.55%) and 44 questions incorrectly (22.45%)"
- Source date / version: 2 Aug 2024; GPT-3.5 / GPT-4.0 snapshot not stated in fetched text.
- Verification status: VERIFIED (via PMC mirror)
- Confidence: high for numbers; medium for the unsupported human-comparison claim (flagged above).

### F8: Cross-language prompting for Polish or Slavic languages: native-vs-English prompting results are mixed; in Is Translation All You Need?, English-translated prompts helped ChatGPT on average, but this study has no Polish.
- Study / domain: Liu et al., "Is Translation All You Need? A Study on Solving Multilingual Tasks with Large Language Models", arXiv 2403.10258v3 (rev. 21 Apr 2025) / general NLP tasks
- Models & scores (Table 1, zero-shot averages across six benchmarks; ChatGPT = gpt-3.5-turbo-1106):

| Condition | High-resource avg | Low-resource avg |
|---|---|---|
| Native-Basic | 50.8 | 40.8 |
| Trans-Google (translated to EN) | 61.4 | 59.0 |
| EN-Basic | 56.4 | 46.0 |

  MGSM (math), ChatGPT: native high-resource 44.4 vs translated 74.7; low-resource 19.4 vs 72.7. Qwen1.5-72B on Chinese NLP tasks: native 62.9, translated 64.2, EN-CoT 70.0.
- Sample / threshold / human baseline: 24 languages in NLP benchmarks; 10 languages in real-user queries; no threshold; no human baseline.
- PL vs EN: No Polish. Slavic languages present: Russian and Bulgarian (NLP benchmarks), Ukrainian (user queries).
- Source (primary): https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10258v3
- Location: Section 2 (NLP tasks), Table 1, Table 3, Section 3 (user queries).
- Verbatim snippet: "translation into English can boost the performance of English-centric LLMs on NLP tasks, it is not universally optimal."
- Verification status: VERIFIED (tables read from HTML; text truncated at 100k of 104k chars, Table 1 fully read)
- Confidence: high for numbers (for this model and these benchmarks); the translation tool used (Google Translate) is part of the condition.

### F9: Latent-language evidence: English is the internal pivot language in Llama-2 middle layers.
- Study / domain: Wendler, Veselovsky, Monea, West, "Do Llamas Work in English? On the Latent Language of Multilingual Transformers", arXiv 2402.10588 (v4, 8 Jun 2024) / mechanistic interpretability
- Models & scores: Not an accuracy study. Llama-2 family. Claims three phases across layers; middle layers give higher probability to the English version of the correct next token than to the input-language version (abstract).
- Sample: non-English prompts, Llama-2 family (exact sizes not in abstract).
- PL vs EN: Not Polish; "non-English prompts"; English is the pivot.
- Source (primary): https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10588
- Verification status: VERIFIED-abstract-only (no numbers in abstract)
- Confidence: medium (mechanistic, not performance)
